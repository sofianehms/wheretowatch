About and privacy

What this site does

It shows in which countries a movie or series is available on Netflix and other services, using official data APIs. Access always depends on the country you are in and on each service’s terms.

Data sent and to whom

Your search text, the chosen title’s identifier and your key go to TMDB (api.themoviedb.org, image.tmdb.org) and, if you added its key, Movie of the Night. They can see your IP address. GitHub hosts the site and logs visits like any host.

Stored on your device

Keys to call the APIs · Preferences (country, service, theme) · Saved results for speed and to save calls · Usage counter of the second source. No cookie, no tracking.
